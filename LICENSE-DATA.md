## What the licence does not cover

- The **underlying documents** that the dataset cites. Court filings, sanctions notices, UN reports, vendor reports and articles belong to their publishers. The dataset quotes them briefly with attribution; reuse of the documents themselves is governed by their own terms.

## Responsibility

The dataset compiles public reporting. It is not a finding of fact about any person. Legal posture is stated per record; a charge is an allegation. If you republish a record, carry its caveats and posture with it.
