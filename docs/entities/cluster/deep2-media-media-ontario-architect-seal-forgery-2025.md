## Source-backed context

- CBC reported that a small Ontario design firm hired a remote worker who was later believed by Ontario Provincial Police to be a North Korean fraudster.
- The worker allegedly forged the seal of architect Stephen Mauro on at least two dozen projects, creating professional, safety, regulatory and liability exposure beyond payroll loss.
- The firm's owner Jason Riedel and Mauro were publicly interviewed; no criminal charge or adjudicated DPRK identity was located in the cited evidence.
