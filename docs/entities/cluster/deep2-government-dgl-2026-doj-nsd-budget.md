## Source-backed context

- DOJ states that from May 2024 through FY 2025, National Security Cyber charged 10 U.S.-based individuals who helped DPRK IT-worker teams generate approximately $25 million in income.
- DOJ states that approximately 525 employer-provided laptops were seized in these and other DPRK IT-worker investigations from U.S.-based facilitator residences.
- The document says DPRK IT-worker teams can generate as much as $3 million annually.
