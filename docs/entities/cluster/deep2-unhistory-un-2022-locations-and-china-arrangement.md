## Caveats

- Individual worker names in annex 81 are intentionally omitted from this file because location-level defensive context is sufficient.

## Source-backed context

- A Member State reported at least 13 DPRK IT workers in Malabo and Oyala, Equatorial Guinea, assessed to have arrived in 2018-2019.
- Annex 70 reported that Mangyongbong International Trade and Economic Development Corporation worked with Dandong Junxing Science and Technology Co. Ltd. to send DPRK IT workers to China around October 2020, with the Munitions Industry Department facilitating the relationship.
