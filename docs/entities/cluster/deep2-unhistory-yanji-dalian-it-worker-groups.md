## Caveats

- China said relevant employment contracts had expired by 22 December 2019 and reported implementation of repatriation obligations.

## Source-backed context

- A Member State reported four Munitions Industry Department-subordinate IT-worker groups operating in Yanji, Jilin, in early 2020; two were closely linked to Yanji Silver Star Network Science and Technology Co. Ltd.
- One 16-worker group at Yanji Development Zone Science and Technology Industrial Park reportedly earned nearly $1 million in 2019; a second 15-worker group was relocated from Cambodia and sponsored for entry by Yanji Silver Star.
- Kim Ryu Song reportedly led a seven-worker team and controlled another five-person team; Kwon Kwang Ho led a separate seven-worker team.
- Ma Tong Hyok was reported as a key Dalian-based IT manager involved in bringing workers to China, and Kim Han-su as a China-based worker and manager of multiple teams; Dalian Xinlu Science and Technology Co. Ltd. was linked to the investigation.
