## Caveats

- Counts represent linked actors, interviews and companies; unique confirmed DPRK nationals and successful infiltrations require separate counts.

## Source-backed context

- Okta's September 2025 analysis linked more than 130 actors to more than 6,500 initial job interviews across more than 5,000 distinct companies through mid-2025.
- Okta said the activity crossed almost every vertical and geography rather than remaining limited to U.S. big technology companies.
