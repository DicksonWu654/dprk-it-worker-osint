## Source-backed context

- Flare reports evidence of recruitment from Iran, Syria, Lebanon, and Saudi Arabia; at least two Iranian recruits received formal US-employer offers, and more than ten recruitment instances were visible.
- Weekly reports documented applications or late-stage hiring at major US defense, telecommunications, enterprise software, cryptocurrency, and financial-sector employers. The public article generally withholds company names.
- Operator code names Sea, Eugene, and Fineboy tracked recruitment, mass applications, interview preparation, and LinkedIn outreach. Sea mapped an Iranian interviewer to the fabricated Jack Long persona and applied to more than 100 C#/.NET roles per day under that profile.
- Flare distinguishes facilitators who manage identities/applications from recruited developers who appear in phone and technical interviews. Recruits are coached and evaluated for technical skill and English fluency.
- A separate confirmed DPRK-controlled machine tied to the previously reported private-network address (redacted) back-office environment exposed a standardized recruiting/onboarding workflow operated under Michael with a co-facilitator called Lander.
- The operation offered staged compensation, cryptocurrency payment, Western working hours, and meeting-only roles, indicating a formalized interview-associate labor model.
