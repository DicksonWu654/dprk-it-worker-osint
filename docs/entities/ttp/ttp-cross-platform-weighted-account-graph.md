## Caveats

- Auto-blocking every neighbor of a flagged account and using demographic or nationality proxies are unsuitable controls.
- Identity-document imagery, synthetic-image examples, biometric templates, candidate-level telemetry, and raw graph nodes stay out of publication and operational use.
- Graph edges require provenance, weighting, explainability, temporal context, and human review; shared résumé structure, employers, repositories, or contact patterns provide correlation context, while identity proof requires additional evidence.
- Identity or attribution requires more than a single face, image, document, VPN, location, device, network, profile, résumé, repository, contact, or behavior signal.
- The package includes published Rapid7 graph context only; inferred labels are excluded.
- Retain source provenance and confidence for every edge and distinguish shared control from ordinary collaboration.
- Successful or failed verification is one bounded observation; legal identity, nationality, DPRK affiliation, worker continuity, present account control and product-wide performance require separate evidence.
- The source-labeled 23 additional applicants remain suspicious review leads; worker, incident, and legal identity status remain unconfirmed.
- Use explainable multi-signal review, minimization, bounded retention, trained human adjudication, correction, appeal, accessibility accommodations, and applicable legal review.

## Source-backed context

- Useful graph edges include repeated code-repository handles, shared projects/code, social relationships, overlapping email-domain patterns, infrastructure reuse, contractor-profile attributes, and front-company accounts. Human review should distinguish a true shared-control signal from ordinary open-source collaboration.
