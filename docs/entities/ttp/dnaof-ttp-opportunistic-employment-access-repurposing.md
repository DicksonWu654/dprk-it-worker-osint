## Source-backed context

- The joint Dutch state-actor assessment says North Korea conducted several cyberattacks against Dutch companies and persons and separately uses IT workers at Western companies to generate revenue. It warns that access or sensitive data obtained through the employment channel can be exploited opportunistically. This supports modeling fraudulent employment as both a sanctions-evasion/revenue risk and a contingent access pathway, without claiming that every worker performs intrusion or theft.
- Scope: Dutch companies and persons; the public source does not name victims or identify a narrower industry in the reviewed passage.
