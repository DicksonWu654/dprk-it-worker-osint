## Source-backed context

- Raney says the controlled identity completed two interviews and received a verbal $70,000 offer for a public-trust position at a U.S. government contractor in the geospatial-intelligence space before he declined and notified the company.
- Apply identity continuity and independent work-history checks before public-trust or sensitive contractor offers, not only after onboarding.
- Include contractors and subcontractors in endpoint, identity, and insider-risk controls.
