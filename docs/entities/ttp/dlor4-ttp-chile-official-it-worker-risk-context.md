## Source-backed context

- Chile's official national risk assessment identifies overseas labor as a DPRK fundraising channel and separately records the IT-worker model: destination-country companies run by DPRK developers or use of false identities, computer-related work abroad, estimated monthly earnings and a remittance share to the DPRK.
- Evidence class: official national risk assessment synthesizing international evidence.
- Original-language support (es): La UAF explica que la RPDC obtiene ingresos mediante trabajadores enviados al exterior. En la nota 56 señala que en el área TI se estimaban ingresos mensuales de USD 3.000 a 5.000, aproximadamente un tercio remitido a la RPDC, y que trabajadores relacionados con informática habían sido enviados a Europa, Asia, África y Medio Oriente utilizando empresas del país de destino dirigidas por desarrolladores de la RPDC o identidades falsas.
- English translation supplied in the research package: Chile's FIU explains that the DPRK raises income through workers sent abroad. Note 56 says IT-area monthly earnings were estimated at USD 3,000-5,000, about one third sent to the DPRK, and that computer-related workers had reportedly been sent to Europe, Asia, Africa and the Middle East using destination-country companies run by DPRK developers or false identities.
