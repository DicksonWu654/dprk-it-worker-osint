## Source-backed context

- In 2024, Malaysia's FIU received a suspicious report from a reporting institution concerning an unnamed Malaysian individual facilitating remote IT workers with possible DPRK linkages.
- The reporting institution observed incoming funds from multiple IT companies and onward transfers to other individuals; the source report anonymizes the relevant foreign jurisdictions as anonymized foreign jurisdictions.
- The same individual appeared to receive employment income from multiple companies.
- Deposits from a payroll provider contained recipient-name mismatches, leading the reporting institution to suspect that the deposits were intended for another third party.
- Other-sector reports identified that the individual held ownership interests in multiple companies.
- The case was still under investigation when the mutual-evaluation report was published.
