## Source-backed context

- The former worker described first posing as Chinese while recruiting people in countries including Hungary and Turkey to lend identities for a share of earnings.
- He then used those identities to approach people in Western Europe, often seeking UK identities for applications to higher-paying US and European employers.
- Workers with stronger English could specialize in applications, while freelancer platforms and text-centric workplace communication reduced face-to-face verification pressure.
