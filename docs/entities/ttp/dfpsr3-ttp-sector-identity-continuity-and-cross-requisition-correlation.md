## Source-backed context

- FS-ISAC describes multiple member reports across both applicant and contractor stages and continuing sharing about impersonated and synthetic identities. This supports controls that preserve identity continuity from application through onboarding and active employment, including contractors and staffing vendors.
- FS-ISAC says two intelligence reports helped one member identify an applicant who had applied to twelve developer and engineering roles. The safe defensive lesson is to correlate applicant identity and application history across requisitions rather than review each application in isolation.
