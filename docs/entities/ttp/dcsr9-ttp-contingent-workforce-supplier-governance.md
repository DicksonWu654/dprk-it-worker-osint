- Nationality, accent, language, appearance, geography, camera behavior, remote-work preference, job title, and any single technical signal require corroboration before attribution or adverse action.

- Use proportionate, documented, privacy-preserving review with a meaningful human appeal path.

## Source-backed context

- Salesforce's contingent-workforce supplier enablement ties layered interview, identity, background and change-request checks to supplier governance: intentional failure to adhere to and adapt the guidance may trigger business review and possible contract termination. The useful cross-sector lesson is to make identity-continuity controls flow down to staffing and contingent-labor suppliers and to establish an enforceable exception and escalation path.
