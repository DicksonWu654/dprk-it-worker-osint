## Source-backed context

- CERT Bulgaria states that DPRK fraudulent IT-worker activity affects European states including Bulgaria.
- The advisory describes false-identity remote hiring, referrals, third-party equipment delivery, substitute payees, avoidance of direct visual contact, legitimate remote-management tooling, and data-theft or extortion risk as generalized warning signs and risks.
- It recommends layered identity verification, video interviews, least-privilege technical access, data minimization, anomaly monitoring, and coordinated HR and information-security awareness.
