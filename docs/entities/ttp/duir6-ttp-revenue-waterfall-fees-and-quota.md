## Source-backed context

- Overseas workers were reported to handle several projects simultaneously; experts estimated typical monthly earnings of $3,000-$5,000 and substantially higher earnings for highly skilled workers.
- Workers reportedly paid 5%-30% of income to proxy-account holders and usually 20%-30% to payment or laundering intermediaries, or a fixed monthly per-person fee.
- After office, equipment, and living expenses, supervisors and managers received a share, the dispatching organization received a share, and funds were handed over to Pyongyang under a designated quota requirement.
