## Caveats

- False-positive caution: Lawful subcontracting and accessibility support also create role separation; review should focus on undisclosed or inconsistent control rather than teamwork itself.
- False-positive caution: Staffing agencies, bootcamps, families, and legitimate consulting teams may share references, templates, addresses, or devices; require several corroborating signals and human review.
- One BBC-observed organization is a single observed example; broader DPRK IT-worker unit coverage requires additional sources.

## Source-backed context

- BBC’s reviewed internal messenger material describes a four-level structure: senior security/discipline management, intermediate work allocators, practical support staff who prepare false resumes/accounts, and outward-facing IT workers. The records also showed role-based allocation of accounts and computers. This is richer than a generic “team manager” tag because it identifies functional separation across the employment-fraud lifecycle.
