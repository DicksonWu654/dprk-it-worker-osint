## Caveats

- The differing revenue figures remain separate; a precise universal rule requires source harmonization.
- These are Member State/former-worker estimates and may reflect different periods, teams or gross-versus-net accounting than S/2019/691 and S/2020/151.

## Source-backed context

- A Member State reported overseas IT-worker earnings of $15,000-$60,000 monthly; a former worker reported an annual expectation of at least $100,000, often through multiple full-time positions plus freelance jobs.
- Former workers reported retaining only a small percentage: approximately 10% for lower earners up to 30% for the highest earners, with dispatching agencies taking the rest and sometimes using it to purchase goods for the DPRK.
- A former worker estimated 3,000 workers overseas and 1,000 inside the DPRK.
