## Source-backed context

- The advisory describes an integrated cyber and IT curriculum coordinated with Workers' Party, research-center, and military priorities. It says approximately 30,000 students studied information and communications technology subjects at three leading universities, that by 2019 dozens of universities had established advanced STEM programs, that promising students were recruited from specialized secondary schools, and that IT workers historically received additional training through overseas placements and regional IT research centers in East Africa, Southeast Asia, and South Asia.
