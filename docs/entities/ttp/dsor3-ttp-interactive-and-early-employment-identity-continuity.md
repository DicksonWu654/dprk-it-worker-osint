## Source-backed context

- ThaiCERT's February 2026 item adds a newer Thai-language source for defending against fraudulent technical applicants. Its narrow delta is not the visual anomalies in the anecdote, but the control sequence: disable virtual backgrounds when appropriate, ask the applicant to perform a simple live interaction with a physical object, and use a lawful early-employment in-person or equivalent identity-continuity check for higher-risk roles.
- AI-generated résumés and false professional profiles can be combined with deepfake video and real-time AI-assisted answers.
- A live interaction challenge can supplement, but not replace, document, employment, device, network and behavioral verification.
- Identity assurance should continue into early employment rather than ending at interview completion.
