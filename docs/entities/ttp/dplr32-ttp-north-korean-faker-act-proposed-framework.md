## Source-backed context

- Coordinate allied sanctions and other restrictive measures against persons knowingly conducting or facilitating DPRK remote-worker schemes.
- Increase allied information sharing about remote-worker schemes, money laundering, and proliferation networks.
- Engage jurisdictions described as hosting, enabling, or failing to prevent the activity and support coordinated counter-evasion strategies.
- Engage relevant private-sector experts and address artificial intelligence, synthetic identities, and other emerging technologies.
- Authorize enhanced Rewards for Justice outreach and public attribution as appropriate.
- Require an initial report after enactment and annual reports for two years.
