## Caveats

- The cited material leaves the affected German employer and worker unidentified.
- Possible broader DPRK-Russia infrastructure overlap is excluded from screening guidance because the material supplies context rather than a worker-attribution rule.
- The information-loss and espionage language is a risk assessment; placement-level exfiltration requires case evidence.

## Source-backed context

- Germany's 2025 domestic-intelligence report states that German companies had already employed concealed DPRK IT specialists, some working directly from the DPRK and others from abroad. It assesses that their deep system access can support sanctions evasion and may also enable sensitive-information loss and DPRK espionage. The report additionally notes possible limited technical overlap between DPRK and Russian cyber activity through shared infrastructure, but does not attribute any worker persona or employer by name.
