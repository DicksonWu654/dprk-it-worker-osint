## Source-backed context

- The Australian Sanctions Office says DPRK IT workers seek Australian remote work while concealing nationality, identity and location through fake personas, proxy accounts, stolen identities and falsified documents. It states Australian businesses have been targeted through mainstream freelance/networking platforms and formal recruitment companies. Its July 31, 2026 update adds AI-assisted interview communication, identity/profile inconsistencies, remote-access and account-location anomalies, independent identity and document verification, multi-channel interviews, role-based access control, software review and payment suspension or access restriction after a supported suspicion.
- The separate November 6, 2025 advisory describes remote workers using stolen identities, fabricated online portfolios, AI image generation and voice alteration, VPNs and legitimate remote-management tooling. It recommends independent identity-document verification, limiting access to role needs, reviewing access logs, scanning for unauthorized software or code changes, restricting remote access after a supported suspicion and assessing what material was accessed.
