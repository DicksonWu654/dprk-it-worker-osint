## Caveats

- Face-to-face checks must be lawful and remain distinct from facial recognition, nationality profiling and biometric matching.
- Sparse professional connections, AI use, phone-region mismatch, and remote work are contextual signals; DPRK affiliation requires independent evidence.
- ThaiCERT's item is localized secondary guidance; actor-specific claims should be traced to the underlying primary disclosures.

## Source-backed context

- ThaiCERT warns Thai readers about suspected DPRK-linked fraudulent remote engineering and software-development applicants. It summarizes resume/account inconsistencies, newly created email addresses, telephone-region mismatch, deepfake interviews and use of ChatGPT during interviews. Its mitigation section stresses cooperation among security, HR and legal teams; controlled device pickup; scrutiny of delivery addresses; face-to-face interviewing where appropriate; and integration of validated applicant-fraud indicators into screening systems.
