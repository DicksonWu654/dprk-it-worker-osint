## Caveats

- One observed account generated only about $1,050 over a year; this is an account-level amount rather than a network-wide estimate.
- The Panel did not identify the platform or publish the sensitive identity details retained in this finding.

## Source-backed context

- The Panel reported use of false identification, VPNs and Hong Kong front companies to evade platform due diligence and know-your-customer checks.
- Most linked accounts in one company investigation operated from China; accounts often moved customers off-platform after contact and targeted platforms with weaker security or due diligence.
- A Jilin-linked account was connected to four additional accounts through a Chinese phone number and IP addresses and used likely fraudulent identity data; the Panel warned of unintentional platform access to international payment systems.
