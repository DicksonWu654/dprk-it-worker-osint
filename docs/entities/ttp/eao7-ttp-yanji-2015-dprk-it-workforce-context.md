## Source-backed context

- Yanji High-Tech Zone reported that it coordinated with the DPRK consulate and DPRK companies concerning the quantity and quality of DPRK IT recruitment.
- The report stated that 14 companies in the zone employed DPRK IT technical personnel and that 312 personnel had been introduced cumulatively during the reported period.
- The employers and workers are unnamed; the aggregate historical count does not identify any front company, facilitator, persona, account, or individual worker.
- Original-language evidence: “高新区积极协调朝鲜领事馆和朝鲜会社，保证朝鲜IT人才引进的数量和质量，全区招用朝鲜IT技术人才企业达到14家，累计引进朝鲜IT技术人才312人。”
- Researcher-supplied English translation: The report said the high-tech zone coordinated with the DPRK consulate and DPRK companies to maintain the quantity and quality of DPRK IT recruitment; 14 companies in the zone employed DPRK IT technical personnel, with 312 personnel introduced cumulatively.
