## Source-backed context

- Using third-party network data, IP geolocation, routing data, and open-source information for March-August 2018, Recorded Future assessed that China, India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Mozambique, Kenya, Thailand, and Indonesia were more likely than Russia to host North Koreans working in service or information-economy roles. The report observed regional activity in China and India and contrasted an internet-dependent, customer-facing model—such as building games, applications, bots, and other IT products—with predominantly manual overseas labor.
