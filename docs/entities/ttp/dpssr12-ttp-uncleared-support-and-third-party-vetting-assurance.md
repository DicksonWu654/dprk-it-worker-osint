## Source-backed context

- Treat uncleared support roles with privileged network or data access as part of the national-security identity-assurance boundary, and include identity-verification requirements in scopes of work for third parties that conduct personnel vetting or background screening. INSA proposes a DCSA-led government-industry working group to compare controls, establish risk-calibrated baselines, and account for remote government and contractor workspaces.
