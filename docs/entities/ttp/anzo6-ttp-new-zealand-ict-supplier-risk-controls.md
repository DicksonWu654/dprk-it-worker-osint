## Source-backed context

- New Zealand Government Procurement identifies ICT goods and services, sensitive data and business-critical functions as national-security-sensitive procurement. It directs agencies to assess suppliers, systems and supply chains; perform regular due diligence; monitor ownership and behavior changes; use security, audit and change-of-control clauses; and document evidence before excluding a supplier on national-security grounds.
