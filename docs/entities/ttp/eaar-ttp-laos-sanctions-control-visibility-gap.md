## Caveats

- The 2024 APG follow-up considered technical compliance changes; operational effectiveness and issue persistence require separate evidence.

- This is regulatory and defensive context only; Laos actor, company, and operating-hub profiles are outside this record.

## Source-backed context

- APG's 2023 evaluation records that DPRK IT workers and other DPRK workers had been reported as hosted in Lao PDR, while Lao PDR could not provide assessors with requested statistics or information on them.
- The assessment says authorities did not demonstrate awareness of sanctions-evasion vulnerabilities associated with reported DPRK nationals remaining after the 2019 repatriation deadline.
- The report identifies practical control weaknesses relevant to detection: delayed sanctions-list notifications, reliance on an English-language website, weak awareness among smaller financial institutions and DNFBPs, and limited supervision and enforcement.
- The APG's 2024 technical follow-up did not reassess effectiveness, so it does not establish that these DPRK-specific operational gaps were resolved.
