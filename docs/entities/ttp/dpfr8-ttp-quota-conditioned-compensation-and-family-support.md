## Source-backed context

- One former-worker account described allowance as contingent on meeting a government-set earnings quota and linked quota success to whether money could be sent to family. Other interview material described client-driven night work, daytime development, sleep disruption, and stress when assignments did not succeed. The material supports a coercion mechanism in which compensation, family support, and work intensity are tied to performance demands.
