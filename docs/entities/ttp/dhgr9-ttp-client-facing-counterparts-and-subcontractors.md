## Source-backed context

- The advisory states that DPRK IT workers often rely on overseas contacts to obtain freelance work and interface with customers. It further describes managers hiring non-DPRK subcontractors or teams—sometimes unaware of the employer's real identity—to complete work, make software purchases, or handle customer interactions that could expose a DPRK worker. Separately, some teams reportedly operated on paper through a legitimate local company or paid a foreign company to conceal their origin.
