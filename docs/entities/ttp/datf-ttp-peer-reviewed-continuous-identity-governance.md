## Caveats

- Behavioral biometrics, location telemetry, digital-footprint review, or unexpected live checks can be intrusive and error-prone; use only where lawful and proportionate, with minimization, human review, accessibility accommodations, and appeal paths.
- Multi-factor authentication remains valuable; the bounded point is that it may be insufficient when an adversary controls the enrolled endpoint or uses a facilitator.
- The article provides governance analysis rather than a controlled effectiveness study of DPRK-specific interventions.
