## Source-backed context

- Raney says a prospective facilitator realized that her tasks resembled a public laptop-farm case. She had been told she was helping Polish developers who were Ukrainian refugees, and the initial recruitment messages came through freelance platforms.
- Train recruiting, contractor, and trust-and-safety teams that facilitator recruitment may be framed as charitable assistance or refugee support.
- Escalate offers to lend identity, receive laptops, create accounts, or move compensation even when the social story appears benevolent.
