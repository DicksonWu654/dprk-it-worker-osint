## Source-backed context

- The Israel National Cyber Directorate describes DPRK workers using false freelance identities, AI-generated or manipulated interview media, fabricated portfolio or employment evidence, and real-time video or audio manipulation. After hiring, the advisory describes use of legitimate RMM products, including AnyDesk, RustDesk and Chrome Remote Desktop, to sustain remote access under the appearance of ordinary work.
