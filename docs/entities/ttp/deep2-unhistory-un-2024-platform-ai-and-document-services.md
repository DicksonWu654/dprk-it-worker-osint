## Caveats

- A platform assessment that most skilled freelance crypto profiles were fake was an opinion and is rejected as too broad for publication.
- AI use, remote work, crypto work, VPN use and one inconsistent profile are contextual signals; attribution requires a broader source-backed pattern.

## Source-backed context

- The Panel compiled reports of falsified resumes, inconsistent cross-platform identities, purchased or borrowed high-reputation accounts, photo-realistic identity-document and supporting-document generators, account-resale services, and proxy email/SMS services.
- DPRK IT workers reportedly used generative AI for profile images, profile text and coding/interview answers; workers increasingly approached cryptocurrency companies and community chats directly when major platforms tightened controls.
- Reports also described seeking web3, blockchain, smart-contract and cryptocurrency work and potentially exploiting or inserting vulnerabilities.
