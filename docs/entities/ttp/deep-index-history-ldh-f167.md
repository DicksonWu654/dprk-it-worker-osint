## Caveats

- Keep shipping addresses and personal reference details out of the record.
- These are risk indicators for proportionate review; proof requires corroboration, and screening must avoid discrimination.

## Source-backed context

- Nisos says suspicious applicants may change the equipment-shipping address after an offer and recommends checking whether the new address is actually linked to the claimed person.
- References may be the same person or part of the same network as applicants, avoid camera, and provide brief answers lacking credible supervisory context.
- Cross-platform persona checks should compare name, appearance, location, work history and education; same-photo/different-location profiles and sparse professional-only footprints are useful correlated signals.
