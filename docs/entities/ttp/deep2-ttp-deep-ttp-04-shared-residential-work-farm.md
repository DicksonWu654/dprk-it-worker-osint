## Caveats

- False-positive caution: Contractors may rotate lawful devices or use virtual desktops. Provide an exception process and avoid invasive home monitoring.
- False-positive caution: Coworking spaces, universities, call centers, shared housing, and managed service providers can create shared infrastructure. A common NAT address can reflect shared infrastructure; identity and device-control inconsistencies are the required additional signals.
- Former-worker testimony is retrospective and approximate; its numeric estimates describe reported scale rather than exact values.
