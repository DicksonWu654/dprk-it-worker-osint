## Source-backed context

- Ashby reports an increase in fake-candidate reports across its team and talent community and describes a sequence combining shallow professional accounts, copied resume content, identity and accent inconsistencies, remote-only constraints, later shipping-address changes, and remote-management software after hire.
- Copy resume material from real professionals
- Build accounts whose depth does not match the claimed career
- Change the equipment shipping address after hire
- Install remote-management software after onboarding
- Complete background and employment verification before shipping equipment or granting access
- Independently validate former employers and references
- Use structured on-camera and live practical steps while allowing accessibility exceptions
- Reconfirm identity and approved location at equipment shipment and first access
