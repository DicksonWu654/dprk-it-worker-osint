## Caveats

- Apply payment review proportionately, document legitimate business explanations, minimize retained data, and provide a correction and escalation path.
- Cryptocurrency use, a payment-information mismatch, or a third-party payee is a contextual signal requiring corroboration; DPRK affiliation, fraud, sanctions status, or wrongdoing comes from corroborated case evidence, and hiring controls use review rather than automatic rejection or blocking.
- The official PDF and publication page returned HTTP 410 during source validation; the exact official URL and locator are retained rather than replaced with an unofficial mirror.

## Source-backed context

- Use traceable employer-approved compensation rather than cryptocurrency for remote IT work, reconcile payment-beneficiary information with the worker's verified identity records, and escalate mismatches for documented human review before payment.
