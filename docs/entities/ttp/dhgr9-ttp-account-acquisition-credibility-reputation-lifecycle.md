## Source-backed context

- The advisory describes proxy accounts used to bid, work, and receive payment; account owners selling or lending account access; multiple identities shared within a team; one dedicated device per account in some cases; fabricated education, employment, portfolio, client, statement-of-work, invoice, and communication records; self-dealing between fraudulent client and developer accounts to build ratings; reused document templates; clusters of accounts receiving ratings from one client; extensive bidding with low acceptance; and recruitment of additional workers after one worker is hired.
