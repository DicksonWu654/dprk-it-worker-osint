## Source-backed context

- The RCMP-led advisory says employing IT workers deployed by the North Korean government could have consequences under Canadian sanctions and directs reporting entities to FINTRAC. FINTRAC's underlying guidance requires sanctions-evasion risk assessment and enhanced measures for transactions with suspicion, an explicit North Korea connection, or origin in or destination to North Korea.
- Evidence class: official national advisory plus primary compliance guidance.
