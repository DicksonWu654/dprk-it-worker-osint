## Caveats

- The official PDF and publication page returned HTTP 410 during source validation; the exact official URL and locator are retained rather than replaced with an unofficial mirror.
- This record adds the explicit extension of DPRK-specific training responsibility to internal HR and contracted staffing firms; generic supplier diligence and hiring controls remain outside it.
- Training and contract controls should be proportionate, privacy-preserving, accessible, and tested for discriminatory or disparate impact, with correction and escalation paths.
- Training indicators are non-exclusive; identity, nationality, sanctions status, fraud, and wrongdoing require independent corroboration. Consequential action requires documented human review.

## Source-backed context

- Give internal human-resources personnel and contracted third-party staffing firms the same DPRK IT-worker-specific interview and hiring-risk training, playbooks, and escalation channels.
