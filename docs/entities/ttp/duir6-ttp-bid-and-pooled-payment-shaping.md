## Source-backed context

- The Panel annex says workers used remote control of a proxy account holder's computer and internal guidance limiting one account to three project bids per day to reduce scrutiny.
- Workers reportedly used multiple accounts because only a fraction of bids generated responses.
- Group payment accounts received proceeds for multiple workers, often in round amounts; operators limited monthly or annual flow and transaction counts, while payments could arrive every several days or multiple times in one day from different sources.
- Funds were reportedly aggregated in third-country financial systems, with at least some cash withdrawal through proxy-controlled accounts.
