## Source-backed context

- New Zealand joined the October 22, 2025 MSMT statement describing fraudulent IT work as part of DPRK cyber and sanctions-evasion activity linked to UN-designated entities and urging awareness and accountability. Separately, MFAT's living sanctions guidance says North Korean nationals are not entitled to work authorisation in New Zealand and that a North Korean national earning income in New Zealand for regime benefit may fall within deportation controls.
